SAMPLE SERVICES AGREEMENT

This is a synthetic example document for demonstrating ClausePilot AI. It is not legal advice.

1. SERVICES
Northstar Studio will provide brand identity services, including two design concepts, one revision round, and delivery of final digital files. The project is expected to begin within 14 days after the client pays the initial deposit.

2. FEES AND PAYMENT
The total project fee is $1,200. The client will pay a $600 deposit before work begins and the remaining $600 within 30 days after delivery. Invoices unpaid after 10 calendar days may accrue a late fee of 1.5 percent per month or the maximum amount permitted by law, whichever is lower.

3. TERM AND TERMINATION
This agreement begins on the effective date and continues for three months. Either party may terminate the agreement for convenience by giving 30 days' written notice. The client must pay for all work completed and approved expenses incurred through the termination date.

4. REVISIONS AND SCOPE
The fee includes one revision round. Additional revisions, new deliverables, or work requested after approval may require a written change order and additional fees. The client must provide timely feedback so the project schedule can be met.

5. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY
The client owns the final approved files after all fees have been paid. Northstar Studio retains ownership of its pre-existing tools, templates, and general creative methods, and grants the client a non-exclusive license to use those elements as embedded in the final files.

6. CONFIDENTIALITY
Each party will protect non-public information received from the other party and use it only to perform this agreement. These duties continue for two years after the agreement ends, except for information that is publicly available through no fault of the receiving party.

7. DATA AND PRIVACY
Northstar Studio will store the materials needed to complete the project in its service systems. The client should not send sensitive personal information unless the parties agree in writing on additional data-protection measures.

8. WARRANTIES AND THIRD-PARTY SERVICES
The client is responsible for confirming that it has permission to use any names, logos, fonts, images, or other materials it provides. Third-party services used for collaboration or delivery are subject to their own terms.

9. LIMITATION OF LIABILITY
To the maximum extent permitted by law, Northstar Studio's total liability under this agreement will not exceed the fees paid by the client under this agreement. Neither party will be liable for indirect, incidental, or consequential damages.

10. CANCELLATION BY PROVIDER
Northstar Studio may cancel the project if the client is more than 10 days late with an undisputed payment, if the requested work violates applicable law, or if continued work would create a security risk. Any refund will be calculated using the work completed and costs already incurred.

11. GOVERNING LAW
This agreement is governed by the laws of the State of California. The parties will first attempt to resolve any dispute through good-faith discussion before filing a lawsuit.

12. ENTIRE AGREEMENT
This document and any written change order signed by both parties contain the complete agreement. It replaces earlier discussions about the project. Changes must be written and accepted by both parties.
